How each version of the rules scores每一版规则的成绩

Each version of the triage rules is run over up to three rounds of submissions whose core documents a person chose by hand, and scored against that person’s answers. Every figure counts documents: one file’s content within one submission, however many copies of it the export holds. 每一版分拣规则最多在三轮提交上跑一遍，再与人工逐份挑出的核心文件比对。所有数字都按文件计：同一份提交里内容相同的文件，导出里不论有几个副本，都只算一份。

How a figure is counted数字怎么算

  • The rules’ answer is the role in the run’s output: CORE is kept; SECONDARY, INTERNAL, DERIVED and WITHDRAWN are dropped; UNDECIDED is no answer.规则的答案就是运行结果里的结论：CORE 算留下；SECONDARY、INTERNAL、DERIVED、WITHDRAWN 算不留；UNDECIDED 算没有答案。
  • Recall can only count documents a person saw. The MVP20 analyst worked from triage output and the TEST15 reviewer from v5’s sheet, so a document triage never surfaced is in no answer of theirs.召回率只数得到人看过的文件。MVP20 的分析师对着分拣结果挑，TEST15 的复核人对着 v5 的表作答，分拣没列出来的文件不在这两轮的答案里。
  • MVP20’s answers carry the project owner’s 34 corrections of 2026-09-30: an email naming the written or signed line is core. Every document v5, 98a0573 and 7b4ec16 miss in MVP20 is one of those emails: 33, 33 and 34 of them.MVP20 的答案含项目负责人 2026-09-30 的 34 处更正：写明 written line 或 signed line 的邮件算核心。v5、98a0573、7b4ec16 在 MVP20 漏掉的全是这类邮件：分别 33、33、34 封。
  • TEST15’s answers start from the v5 sheet: where the reviewer changed nothing, the answer is v5’s call, or a drop where v5 made none. Of the 74 calls the reviewer changed, d09e319 follows 11, b9140d4 19 and 7e6db6b 20.TEST15 的答案以 v5 表为底：复核人没改的，答案就是 v5 的结论；v5 没下结论的，记为不留。复核人改过的 74 条里，d09e319 跟上 11 条，b9140d4 19 条，7e6db6b 20 条。
  • From d09e319 to b9140d4 the extra keeps rose by 56 in MVP20, 1 in MVP3 and 16 in TEST15. Of the documents that became extra keeps, 58, 5 and 18 are emails b9140d4’s email rule keeps for the line or the firm order terms they carry, which the person dropped. MVP20’s and MVP3’s count as extra keeps until the project owner rules on those answers.从 d09e319 到 b9140d4，多留的文件在 MVP20 增加 56 份，MVP3 增加 1 份，TEST15 增加 16 份。新变成多留的文件里，分别有 58、5、18 份是 b9140d4 的邮件规则因邮件自己写明承保份额或 FOT 而留下、人却没留的邮件。MVP20、MVP3 的这些邮件，在项目负责人对这些答案作出裁定之前，都算多留。
  • From b9140d4 to 7e6db6b no document a person kept moved. The undecided fell by 38 in MVP20, 1 in MVP3 and 15 in TEST15, and the extra keeps by 8 in MVP20 and 5 in TEST15, every one of them a document the person dropped.从 b9140d4 到 7e6db6b，人留下的文件一份都没动。没下结论的文件在 MVP20 减少 38 份，MVP3 减少 1 份，TEST15 减少 15 份；多留的文件在 MVP20 减少 8 份，TEST15 减少 5 份，全是人没留的文件。
